About The System

One system for everything HR and payroll — built specifically for Nigeria

OKV Nigeria Payroll & HR Management System is a complete workspace for running payroll, managing employees, tracking leave and staff loans, and staying compliant with Nigerian statutory requirements — PAYE, Pension, NHF, and Life Assurance — all calculated automatically using current rates and bands.

It's built around a simple idea: your business shouldn't stop working just because the internet does. The system runs fully offline on any device, and quietly syncs to the cloud the moment you're back online — so your team always has access, wherever they are.

How It Works

Offline-first, cloud-synced — the best of both

The system is designed so a weak connection or a power outage never stops payroll from running.

Offline

Every action saves to your device instantly

  • Add employees, run payroll, and approve leave with zero internet connection
  • Data is stored securely on your device and never lost between sessions
  • Install it once as an app — opens instantly from your Home Screen or Desktop
  • Nothing is blocked while offline — the full system stays usable
Online

Syncs automatically the moment you reconnect

  • Changes push to the cloud automatically — no manual export/import
  • A Sync button and status indicator show exactly what's synced
  • Your team sees each other's updates the moment they're back online
  • Your organization's data is completely private — never visible to other companies
