30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you subscribe to a paid plan and, within 30 days of your first payment, decide the Service isn't right for your Organization, we'll refund that payment in full — no questions asked. This is the same guarantee shown on our Pricing page.

1. 30-day money-back guarantee

  • Applies to your first paid payment on a new subscription (not to trials, which are already free — see Section 2).
  • Covers the full amount paid for that billing period.
  • To claim it, contact us within 30 days of the payment date (Section 3). We'll process the refund back to the account/method you paid from — since payments are by bank transfer, this means a transfer back to your Organization's bank account, typically within 5–10 business days.
  • Once refunded, your account reverts to the free trial state (or is suspended if your trial has already been used) until you choose to subscribe again.

2. Free trial — no charge

Every new Organization gets a 7-day free trial with no payment required and nothing to cancel — if you don't upgrade, your trial simply ends and your account is suspended (your data is kept, not deleted). There's never a refund needed for the trial itself, because nothing is ever charged during it.

3. How to request a refund

Email or WhatsApp us with your Organization name and the date of payment:

We'll confirm your refund within 1 business day and process payment back to your Organization's bank account within 5–10 business days.

4. Cancelling your subscription

  • You can cancel at any time by contacting us (Section 3/8) — there's no lock-in contract and no cancellation fee.
  • If you cancel outside the 30-day guarantee window, you keep access for the remainder of the period you've already paid for; we don't pro-rate or refund partial unused periods outside the guarantee.
  • After your paid period ends, your account is suspended (not deleted) rather than auto-charged again, since renewals are by manual bank transfer, not automatic card billing.

5. Plan changes & downgrades

You can request an upgrade or downgrade at any time from the Upgrade & Subscription section of your dashboard. Upgrades take effect once your new payment is confirmed. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period, so you keep the plan you already paid for until then.

6. What isn't covered

  • Payments older than 30 days (outside the guarantee window) are non-refundable, except where required by law.
  • We can't refund a payment that was never confirmed on our end — please make sure your payment screenshot is uploaded and confirmed in-app; contact us if a genuine payment isn't reflected.
  • Bank transfer fees charged by your own bank when sending payment or receiving a refund are outside our control.

7. Your data after cancellation

Cancelling or a subscription lapsing does not delete your Organization's data. It's retained for up to 90 days (see our Privacy Policy) in case you want to resume, and can be exported on request. If you'd like your data permanently deleted sooner, let us know and we'll action it.

This policy works alongside our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy — if anything here seems to conflict with those, this page governs specifically for refunds/cancellations.
