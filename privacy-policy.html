1. Your role and ours

The Service is HR/payroll software that your organization ("Organization", "you", the subscribing business) uses to process its own employees' data. In NDPA terms:

  • Your Organization is the Data Controller for all employee/staff data you enter into the Service — you decide what data to collect and why, and you're responsible for having a lawful basis (typically the employment relationship) to process it.
  • OKV acts as the Data Processor — we process that data only on your instructions, to provide the Service (storage, calculations, payslips, reports), and we do not use your employees' data for our own purposes.
  • For your own account details (organization name, your name, email, phone, login credentials, billing/subscription history), OKV is the Data Controller.

3. How we use data

  • To provide the Service: running payroll calculations (PAYE, pension, NHF, CRA), generating payslips and reports, managing leave/loan registers, and syncing data across your organization's devices.
  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access within your Organization.
  • To operate your subscription: trial tracking, plan/billing status, manual payment confirmation, renewal reminders.
  • To send account and service-related communications (welcome emails, trial/renewal reminders, capacity or security notices).
  • To provide customer support when you contact us.
  • To maintain the security, integrity and availability of the Service (e.g. detecting suspicious login activity).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your employees' data to serve advertising.

4. Where and how data is stored

The Service is built on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script. Each subscribing Organization gets its own dedicated, private Google Spreadsheet for its transactional data (employees, payroll, messages), separate from every other Organization's spreadsheet. A small central registry (not containing payroll data) tracks which spreadsheet belongs to which Organization. Data is hosted on Google's infrastructure, which maintains its own industry-standard physical and technical security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2/3, and others — see Google's own security documentation for details).

The Service also runs offline: your browser stores a local, on-device copy of your Organization's data (via IndexedDB) so the app keeps working without internet access, syncing back to your Organization's spreadsheet once you're online.

6. When we share data

We do not share your Organization's data with third parties except:

  • With the sub-processors listed above, strictly to operate the Service.
  • Where required by Nigerian law, court order, or a lawful request from a regulatory authority (e.g. the Nigeria Data Protection Commission).
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of OKV, our users, or the public (e.g. investigating fraud or abuse of the Service).
  • With your explicit consent.

Within your Organization's own account, data visibility follows the roles your Admin assigns (e.g. a Payroll Officer sees payroll data; a User typically sees only their own portal). Super Admin (OKV) access is limited to what's needed for platform operation and support — see the capacity-monitoring note below.

OKV's platform-level Super Admin dashboard can see organization-level metadata (org name, plan, subscription status, and spreadsheet storage-capacity percentage) for every subscribing Organization, for billing and system-health purposes. It does not display individual employee payroll records — those remain inside each Organization's own dedicated spreadsheet.

7. Data retention

  • Your Organization's data is retained for as long as your subscription is active, plus a reasonable period afterward to allow reactivation, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • If your account is cancelled or lapses, we retain your data for up to 90 days in case you wish to resume your subscription, after which it may be permanently deleted upon request or as part of routine cleanup.
  • You may request export or deletion of your Organization's data at any time by contacting us (see Section 15).
  • Session tokens and password-reset tokens expire automatically and are not retained beyond their validity window.

8. Security measures

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — only a cryptographic hash is stored.
  • Each Organization's transactional data lives in its own isolated spreadsheet, not a shared table, reducing the risk of cross-organization data exposure.
  • Session tokens expire and are revoked immediately when an account is suspended.
  • Role-based access control restricts what each User can see and do within an Organization's account.
  • All Service traffic runs over HTTPS.
  • We monitor each Organization's storage usage proactively so no account silently runs into platform limits.

No system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately (Section 15) and change your password.

9. Your rights as a data subject

Under the NDPA, individuals whose data we process (as Controller, for account/billing data — or via your Organization, as Controller, for employee data) have the right to:

  • Be informed about how their data is processed (this Policy).
  • Access a copy of the personal data held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of their data, subject to legal/record-keeping obligations (e.g. statutory payroll retention rules).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability, where technically feasible.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

If you are an employee whose data is held in an Organization's account, please contact your employer (the Data Controller) first — they administer your record. If you are an Organization Admin, contact OKV directly using Section 15.

10. Cookies & local storage

The Service does not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies. It uses:

  • Local/session storage in your browser to keep you logged in between visits and to remember display preferences.
  • IndexedDB to store an offline, on-device copy of your Organization's data so the app works without internet access.
  • Service worker caching so the app shell loads instantly and can be installed to your home screen (Progressive Web App).

These are functional, not tracking, technologies — they don't leave your device or get shared with advertisers. Clearing your browser's site data will log you out and remove the offline cache (your data remains safe on your Organization's server-side spreadsheet).

11. Children's data

The Service is a business tool for organizations to manage adult employees and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about individuals under 18 through the Service, other than incidental references an Organization might store (e.g. a staff member's next-of-kin details, entered at that Organization's discretion).

12. Data breach notification

If we become aware of a data breach affecting your Organization's data, we will notify the affected Organization's Admin without undue delay, and, where required by the NDPA, notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, so that appropriate steps can be taken.

13. International data transfer

Because the Service runs on Google's global infrastructure, data may be processed on servers located outside Nigeria. Google maintains recognized international data-protection safeguards for such transfers. By using the Service, you acknowledge this cross-border processing, which is necessary to deliver the Service.
